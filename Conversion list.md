## 🔁 Units That Can Be Converted

The temperature converter supports the following conversions:

- Celsius to Fahrenheit  
- Celsius to Kelvin  
- Fahrenheit to Celsius  
- Fahrenheit to Kelvin  
- Kelvin to Celsius  
- Kelvin to Fahrenheit  

These conversions allow switching between the three main temperature measurement units: **°C**, **°F**, and **K**.
